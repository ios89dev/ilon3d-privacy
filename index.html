1. Who we are

“Ilon 3D” (the “Game”) is a mobile snake game published by ios89 (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game and the third-party services it uses may collect, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Contact: i.o.s892110@gmail.com

2. Age requirement

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will help remove any related data.

3. What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress, coins, settings and scores are stored locally on your device.

The following information is collected automatically by third-party SDKs that the Game includes:

4. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Game.
  • To show advertisements (including rewarded and interstitial ads) that keep the Game free.
  • To measure performance, detect crashes and fix bugs.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions) the Game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later in the Game’s settings where the option is shown.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → delete or reset your advertising ID / opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings.
  • You can also stop all data collection by uninstalling the Game.

6. Data sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, who process it on our behalf or under their own policies, and when required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app or its data. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to the retention settings of the services above. To request access to, or deletion of, data associated with you, e-mail us at the address above with your device/app-instance details (or reset your advertising ID as described in section 5).

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Google and Firebase may process data in countries other than your own, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last changed.
